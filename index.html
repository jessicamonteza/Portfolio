Some of my Projects

Bidermans | UX/UI

Bidermans is a long-standing equestrian supply business rooted in Sweden’s trotting community. To bring that warmth online, they needed their first website and webshop. My role was UX/UI designer, covering the design system, screens, prototype, and responsive WordPress development.

Kvantab | Branding

Kvantab works with political information and public policy, using AI to simplify complex topics. Their identity lacked a clear visual system and warmth. As part of a three-person team, I focused on visual design, from exploration and moodboards to typography, colour, and the final brandbook.

Boj Roslagen | Marknadsförign stategy

Brottsofferjouren Roslagen offers free, confidential support to crime victims, but their services were not always clear. During a Change Management course at Hyper Island, our team developed a strategy to reach more victims. My focus was marketing strategy, including mapping and prioritizing local partners.

Sage | Human centered design

Maria Ungdom supports young people through treatment and recovery, where take-home assignments are important. Since these were paper-based, they were easy to forget or lose. Working in a four-person team, I focused on the visual design and personality of Sage, including its character and persona illustration.

A bit about me

I'm a multidisciplinary designer based in Stockholm, focusing on UX/UI, Branding and Marketing. Growing up between Swedish and Peruvian culture gave me an intuition for people, empathy, and picking up on what's left unsaid. It's a perspective I carry into how I design.

I studied Digital Creative at Hyper Island, where I learned to bridge big-picture strategy with intuitive design. My process is research-led and iterative, always testing, refining, and asking whether an experience truly works for the people using it. Having worked in logistics before design, I naturally see the bigger picture and make sure a brand's story translates into something that works in practice.

Outside of work, you'll find me painting, baking, working with clay, or testing a new recipe. I have a deep love for animals, and my cat Billie often finds her way into my more playful projects.
